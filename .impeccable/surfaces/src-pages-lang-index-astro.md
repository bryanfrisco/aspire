# Surface brief: Home (and site shell)

Scope: home page plus the shared shell (header, footer, page heads) that every page inherits. Visitor mode: Persuade (home); inner pages are Read (governance, news, sustainability) or Operate (reports, contact) inside the same world.

Audience: offtakers and partners, Astra/UT group stakeholders, regulators and North Konawe communities, job seekers, press. Many are on mobile or on slow connections.
Job and action: understand that ASPIRE is an integrated nickel company (mine to RKEF to industrial park to battery materials) in North Konawe, then go to Operations or send a business enquiry.
Proof: real site photography (aspire.id), the corrected timeline, the RKEF capacity and contractor, and the entity list. Everything else is `[TO UPDATE]`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Green nickel as land in custody. The page is cut into the same red laterite ground as the mine, in stepped benches, and turns green bench by bench from mine to metal to restoration. It refuses the drone-shot hero with navy sans, KPI counters and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Laterite red (#9c3b1e) drenches hero and bench bands; ochre subsoil and shade-net black carry structure; tag aluminium (#e6e8e3) is the reading ground. Fresh-leaf green appears only on the one focused or active element. Stamped aluminium nursery tags (rounded-corner plate, punched hole, number, entity, stage) are the component family. Contour lines are the rule system. Status uses line form: solid means operating, dashed means construction, hairline means planned.

STORY: The visitor sees real red earth meeting forest and sea, reads mined, processed, restored, understands the six operations as one chain in one district, believes it through real sites and provenance lines, and goes to Operations or Enquiry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed site aerial (Molore coast), with its lower third cut by three stepped contour benches in laterite red. A huge stencil-weight headline sits across the benches. The six operations are stamped tags hung along the lowest bench edge, each a link. The two actions, Explore operations and Business enquiry, sit at the bench's left end. The header sits on the photo. A provenance line runs bottom right.

FORM: Benches & Nursery Tags, position 7 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 2070a8f0 (re-roll 1).

Raises: phases are labelled states with pattern (from Cyclorama); green is reserved for focus (from Understory); every dense section opens with a fast one-line stamped summary (from Wildstyle); one continuous terrain, with sections as benches, not stacked cards (from Ebru).

Signature interaction: a contour progress rule. As the page scrolls, the active section's bench contour fills from laterite to leaf green, so restoration is the read position. It is driven by one scroll timeline, and it is static under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
