ASPIRE Stargate has launched its new corporate website in Indonesian and English.

The site brings together our six lines of operation in North Konawe, from the South Block mine to the planned nickel battery materials, with dedicated pages for the environment, occupational health and safety, community development, governance and a reports library.

The site is built to stay light, so it remains fast over mobile networks at the mine site and in the regions.
