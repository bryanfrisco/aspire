Construction of PT Stargate Mineral Asia’s Rotary Kiln Electric Furnace (RKEF) smelter in Molore Village, Langgikima District, North Konawe continues, with production targeted for 2027.

The two-line smelter is being built by Sinosteel Equipment & Engineering Co. Ltd. with a design capacity of 130,740 tons of nickel pig iron (NPI) per year from 1.65% Ni saprolite feed supplied by PT Stargate Pasific Resources.

Power for the smelter is planned to come from the PLN Sulawesi grid rather than a new coal-fired plant, in line with ASPIRE’s commitment to greener nickel.
