PT Danusa Tambang Nusantara (DTN), a subsidiary of PT United Tractors Tbk, acquired PT Anugerah Surya Pacific Resources (ASPIRE), the holding company for nickel mining and related activities in North Konawe, Southeast Sulawesi.

With the acquisition, ASPIRE and its subsidiaries, including PT Stargate Pasific Resources and PT Stargate Mineral Asia, became part of the Astra group.

The acquisition expands United Tractors’ portfolio in the nickel sector as part of its sustainable diversification strategy, and continues the development of Stargate from a mine into an integrated nickel processing centre.
