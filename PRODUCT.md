## Platform

## Stack

Astro, static output only (no SSR). Interactive components (islands) only where needed, e.g. the concession area map. Must deploy as plain static files to any host: Nginx, cPanel, or Cloudflare Pages.

## Users

Business and institutional audiences of a commercial nickel mining company. The page list implies several audiences (inferred, not yet confirmed): prospective offtakers, buyers and partners (nickel products), Astra/United Tractors group stakeholders and lenders (governance, reports), regulators and host communities in North Konawe (K3, ESG, CSR), job seekers (careers), and press (news). Many will browse on mobile and on slow connections.

## Product Purpose

Aspire is the corporate website of **PT Anugerah Surya Pacific Resources (ASPIRE Stargate)**, an integrated nickel company in North Konawe, Southeast Sulawesi. It replaces the current aspire.id, a Squarespace site that is English-only, thin, and looks rigid: it does not read as a mining company or as corporate. The new site must meet the standard of a serious mining corporate website and be **complete**. Every section such a site needs is present, filled with real content where it exists and clearly marked `[TO UPDATE]` placeholders where it doesn't. The owner will supply content once the site meets their bar.

## Positioning

ASPIRE's own stated ambition (from aspire.id) is to become a world-class **green nickel producer** and a national leader in mining and processing, with an ethical, social, and environmental commitment. Its distinguishing structure is vertical integration in one district: mine (South Block), exploration (North Block), mining services, an RKEF smelter, an industrial park, and planned battery-materials phases, all in North Konawe. It is a member of the Astra group.

## Product Principles

1. Complete before polished in content: every section a mining corporate site needs exists, and gaps are visible as `[TO UPDATE]`, never invented.
2. Credibility over flourish: claims need real evidence.
3. Performance is part of the brief: minimal JavaScript, light assets, usable on slow mobile connections.
4. Indonesian and English are equal citizens.
5. Safety, environment, and community content stands level with commercial content.
6. Static and portable: nothing may require a server runtime.
